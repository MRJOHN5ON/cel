## Install

1. Download **Cel-Pro-…-arm64.dmg** below (Apple Silicon Macs, macOS 12.3 or newer).
2. Open the DMG and drag **Cel Pro** into **Applications**.
3. Open Cel Pro. macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because the app isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate.
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the Cel Pro message.
   - Confirm with your password. You only need to do this once.

No Python or other installs needed. The first time you use **BRIA RMBG 2.0** (the default, highest-quality model), Cel Pro downloads it once (about 1 GB). After that everything runs offline.

`.sha256` file: checksum for verifying the download (`shasum -a 256 -c Cel-Pro-*.dmg.sha256`).
